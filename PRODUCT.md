# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Indian intercity bus travellers booking on mobile or desktop, often comparing routes, schedules, fares, amenities, and seat availability under time pressure. Their primary job is to find a trustworthy trip, choose seats with confidence, complete a booking, and retrieve or manage the ticket afterward.

## Product Purpose

VoyageBus is a dependable bus-booking application for route discovery, natural-language search, trip comparison, seat selection, booking, and cancellation. Success means the booking flow feels clear and credible while the existing backend rules remain visible and trustworthy.

## Brand Personality

Calm, dependable, refined. Confident, efficient, and premium without becoming exclusive, theatrical, or decorative.

## Anti-references

Do not resemble a generic AI-generated landing page. Avoid fabricated statistics and testimonials, repetitive card grids, excessive badges, oversized marketing copy, ornamental gradients, glassmorphism, gratuitous page-load animation, and decorative elements that do not help a traveller make a decision. Preserve the composed, editorial character of the earlier prototype while giving VoyageBus its own consistent identity.

## Design Principles

- Build trust through clear trip information, stable hierarchy, and predictable controls.
- Keep the booking task visually dominant; brand expression supports rather than competes with it.
- Improve the existing product incrementally, preserving its calm editorial character and proven backend flows.
- Use authentic travel context and real product data instead of invented social proof or filler content.
- Reserve delight for meaningful moments such as swapping cities, selecting seats, opening filters, and confirming a booking.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG AA contrast. Provide clear keyboard focus states, semantic labels, touch-friendly targets, and reduced-motion alternatives. Seat and booking states must never rely on color alone. Keep transitions between 150 and 220 milliseconds and limit motion to state changes, feedback, loading, drawers, seat selection, and booking confirmation.
